Privacy Policy

At pineapp.win, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you play our games and visit our website, how that data is handled, and your choices regarding cookies and personalized advertising.

✦ Effective Date: September 2026 ✦ Last Updated: September 6, 2026 ✦ Contact: privacy@pineapp.win

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the website located at pineapp.win and all playable web games hosted thereon (including Chrono Pendulum and Edge Rush). By accessing or playing games on pineapp.win, you acknowledge and agree to the data practices described in this statement.

2. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization and do not require user account registration to play any game. We may interact with the following categories of information:

  • Offline Local Storage (Client-Side Only): To deliver continuous arcade features, our games save your personal high scores, shield states, and sound toggle preferences directly in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your computer or phone and is never sent to our servers.
  • Server Log Files: Like standard web platforms, our hosting infrastructure logs non-personally identifiable requests including IP addresses, browser user agent, referring/exit pages, operating system, and date/time stamps. This information is used strictly to diagnose technical faults and maintain server security.
  • Direct Communications: If you reach out to us via our Contact page or via email, we receive the name, email address, and message contents you provide to respond to your inquiry.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

Notice Regarding Third-Party Advertising: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to pineapp.win or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its advertising partners to serve personalized advertisements to you based on your visits to pineapp.win and other websites across the web.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You have complete control over how Google and other ad networks serve ads to you:

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the AboutAds.info Consumer Choices page or the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out portal.
  • You can also configure your web browser to reject cookies or notify you whenever a cookie is set. Note that opting out of personalized advertising does not eliminate ads; it simply serves context-based, non-personalized advertising instead.

4. Web Analytics (Google Analytics)

pineapp.win utilizes Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand aggregate, anonymized website traffic (such as overall page views, popular games, device categories, and general geographical trends). Google Analytics sets cookies to gather this telemetry.

To prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics across all websites, you can install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a web server. We and our third-party service providers (such as Google AdSense and Google Analytics) use cookies to analyze site traffic, prevent fraudulent behavior, and serve relevant advertising.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management for specific web browsers can be found on each browser's official support website.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children is paramount. pineapp.win is a general-audience website offering arcade-style browser games and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal details to us without consent, please contact us at privacy@pineapp.win, and we will promptly delete such information from our records.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / European Economic Area)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to specific rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access: Request copies of personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restrictions on how your data is processed.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@pineapp.win.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to:

  • Know what categories and specific pieces of personal data are collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected from them.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. (pineapp.win does not sell personal information).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising their CCPA rights.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption across all pineapp.win endpoints, to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of web traffic data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new game releases, regulatory updates, or changes in third-party services. The updated version will always be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact our privacy team:
