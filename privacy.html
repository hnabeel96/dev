Privacy Policy

We value your privacy, simplicity, and transparency. This policy outlines how information is handled across pineapp.win.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our games, monitor site performance, and discover which experiences players enjoy most. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, approximate session duration, device type, and operating system. No personally identifiable information is collected by our analytics configuration.

Google Analytics utilizes cookies and anonymous identifiers to measure interactions. You can learn more about how Google handles information and how to opt out by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms.

Advertising & Cookies

To keep pineapp.win free and support future game creation, we display or may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense). Third-party vendors and ad networks, including Google, use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites across the web.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser preferences. To opt out of personalized Google advertising, visit Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Future Updates

If we introduce new advertising partners, analytics platforms, or cookie technologies, this Privacy Policy will be promptly updated here to reflect any changes.
