ABOUT PINEAPP.WIN

Who Runs This Site

pineapp.win is an independent web games lab built and maintained by a solo creator. Every game is handcrafted from scratch with an emphasis on clean code, responsive controls, snappy physics, and zero bloat.

The goal is simple: create delightful, frictionless browser games that load instantly and fit perfectly into a five-minute break.
