pineapp.win is an independent home for quick, original browser games. The site is run by a small creator-led team focused on making playful experiences that load quickly, work in the browser, and fit neatly into a five-minute break.

Privacy and measurement

Google Analytics is used to understand general site activity, such as which pages are visited and how the games are discovered. This helps improve performance and the player experience. The site may add advertising in the future; if that happens, relevant advertising technologies, cookies, and their choices will be explained in an updated privacy notice.
