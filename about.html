ABOUT PINEAPP.WIN

Small games.
Big care.

pineapp.win is run independently by a small creator-led team that makes quick, original browser games. The goal is simple: create playful experiences that load quickly, work in the browser, and fit neatly into a five-minute break.

Analytics and future advertising

Google Analytics helps us understand general site activity, such as which pages are visited and how players discover the games. This information helps improve performance and the player experience.

Advertising may be introduced in the future. If advertising, advertising cookies, or similar technologies are added, this page and the site's privacy information will be updated to explain what is used and what choices are available.
