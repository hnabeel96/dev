Frequently Asked Questions

Are the games on pineapp.win completely free to play?

Yes, 100%. Every game on pineapp.win is freely accessible directly in your web browser. There are no subscriptions, pay-to-win mechanics, or locked levels.

Do I need to install an app or create an account?

No. pineapp.win operates with zero required installs and zero registrations. Simply open any game page and start playing immediately.

How are my high scores and audio settings saved?

Your high scores and sound settings are saved directly in your browser's local storage (localStorage). They stay securely on your personal device and persist across visits as long as you do not clear your browser cache.

Can I play these games on smartphones and tablets?

Yes! All our games are built with responsive layouts and touch-optimized controls supporting iOS Safari, Android Chrome, and modern mobile browsers.

How does pineapp.win support ongoing development?

We display minimal, non-intrusive web advertising (via Google AdSense) on selected landing areas. We never inject mid-action popups or deceptive ad placements that interfere with gameplay.
