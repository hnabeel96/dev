Google Analytics

ANONYMOUS USAGE

We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand how visitors interact with our games, monitor site reliability, and discover which experiences players enjoy most. Analytics gathers aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics such as pages viewed, general session durations, screen resolutions, browser families, and operating systems.

Google Analytics utilizes anonymous identifiers and cookies to evaluate site traffic. No personal identities, email addresses, or payment credentials are collected through our analytics setup.
